# Golden Willow

_From his unit’s window, Dwight Horton scowled at the squirrels helping themselves to the seeds that had been placed in the feeder for the benefit of the birds and not some selfish rodents! “Hey, Jerkwad!” he yelled at the largest one, for that was its name. He knew all their names; Jerkwad, Buttmunch, Idiot, and Fifi; not just because he was the one that named them (though he did) but also because his memory wasn’t nearly as bad as Mrs. Davis kept telling the staff whenever she got the chance. “Get away from there! That’s not for you!” Jerkwad heard him, of course. It was hard not to. But he was a squirrel and didn’t care._

_Dwight was still mid-rant when there was a commotion at the feeder and the squirrels dispersed frantically. A large, lone crow landed on the feeder. It looked Dwight right in eye holding the gaze for a moment before munching the morsels in the feeder. Between gulps the crow would pause and stare at Dwight._

_”You again, eh?“ The crow didn’t have a name, but Dwight knew all about him. Marcus Whats-His-Name from across the hall had told everyone at Bridge how if you made eye contact with a crow, it could steal your soul. Some people thought it was only a rumor, but just two months ago, 98 year old Eileen Bitterbee had made eye contact with a crow and then died only three weeks later. What more proof did one need?! ”You’re not getting me this time, either! Go get Nancy next door! NANCY! DAVIS! N! A! N!...” but the crow took off before he could finish giving directions._

_Letting out a big sigh, Dwight looked around his suite remembering what it had looked like in the old days. Back in the day Golden Willow was the biggest (and only) mall in town. He’d spent a lot of time in this building when he was a teenager and knew it like the back of his hand. The mall had gone bankrupt after all those deaths and the rumors claiming that it was haunted ‘with the souls of the dead!’ It then laid fallow for a couple decades before someone had the bright idea to turn it into a retirement home. Dwight was one of the first to buy-in (to the frustration of his grandkids) so he had his choice of units. He picked this one because it had been the “Galaxy of Fun Arcade” back in his day and was right down the hall from the old food court which was now the dining hall and activity room._

_It was time for Bingo and Dwight had his eye on that set of Ginsu knives in the prize pool. He still had the last set he’d won, but the chef’s knife was still missing after the incident with the alien dopplegangers and was in an evidence locker someplace._

## Inspirations

First and foremost, this game is ~~a blatant rip-off of~~ a loving homage to the [Green Oaks RPG](https://ks.aresgames.eu/product/green-oaks/) by Roberto de Luca.

The Green Oaks game, itself, lists the following inspirations:
* [Red](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1245526)
* [Bubba ho-tep](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0281686)
* [Buffy the Vampire Slayer](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0118276)

And I would add:
* [The Thursday Murder Club](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt12001534)
* [The Old Man & The Gun](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2837574)
